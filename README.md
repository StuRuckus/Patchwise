# Patchwise

A free, browser-based **quilt designer**. Design quilts in classic **square** or modern **hexagon**
styles, fill them with solid colors or photos of your own fabric, use symmetry tools, and get a
fabric **yardage and cutting estimate** — all in your browser, with nothing to install.

**▶ Live app: https://sturuckus.github.io/Patchwise/**

On iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for an app-like icon.

## Features

- **Two styles** — classic square blocks, or hexagons (Grandmother's Flower Garden, tumbling blocks, hex stars, half-hexes, and more).
- **A library of quilt blocks** — half-square triangles, flying geese, log cabin, pinwheel, snowball, four-color quarter-square triangles and four-patches, and others — each with up to four fabric slots.
- **Real fabrics** — solid colors you mix yourself, or **photos of actual fabric** tiled at real-world scale so the print lines up across the whole quilt.
- **Symmetry tools** — mirror, kaleidoscope, and 6-way / 12-way "snowflake" rotation. Design one corner and watch the rest fill in.
- **Fabric planning** — a yardage estimate and a strip-by-strip **cutting guide**, including backing, batting, and binding.
- **Touch-friendly** — pinch to zoom, two-finger pan, tap or drag to edit. Export your design as a **PNG**.
- **Save your work** — name and keep designs right in your browser, or back them up to a file.
- **Works offline** once it has loaded.

## How it works

Patchwise is a single self-contained `index.html` file with no dependencies and no server.
Everything — including your saved designs — runs and stays in your own browser, so it costs nothing
to host and nothing to run.

## Run it yourself

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or drop it on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify,
Cloudflare Pages, etc.). To update the live site, replace `index.html` in this repository.
